To protect the volatile natural essential oils and aromas:
  • Store spices in a cool, dry, and hygienic place away from direct sunlight or heat sources.
  • Ensure the pouch is tightly resealed after every use, or transfer spices into airtight glass/ceramic containers.
  • Avoid using wet spoons to scoop spices as moisture can trigger spoilage.
